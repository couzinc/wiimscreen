Everything you play, one touch away

These are recordings of the real app, made with its simulator.

Tiles

Touch an album. It plays.

Your WiiM presets and the albums you save with the heart sit under the cover, with their art. A ring turns on the tile until the WiiM starts.

  • Presets are read from the WiiM
  • The heart saves the album or playlist that plays
  • The tiles you play most come first
Library

Rate, pin, find.

Hold a tile to give it one to five stars or pin it at the front. The Library shows every tile, best rated first, with filters for presets, favorites, Qobuz and Deezer.

  • Stars follow the station, not the preset number
  • Your Qobuz and Deezer favorites are one touch away
Search

Type it. Touch it. Listen.

Search Qobuz tracks or the Deezer catalog on the on-screen keyboard. A result plays inside its album, so the next track is the right one.

  • Your WiiM searches with its own account
  • The music you had comes back after the search
  • Optional voice search
Ambient view

Readable from the sofa.

After a minute, or with the full-screen button, the cover and the title fill the screen. The colors come from the album art. One touch brings the controls back.

  • Optional sound spectrum from the Tab5's microphones
  • The screen dims and sleeps on its own

Updates itself

New versions install from GitHub on the device. A version that does not start is rolled back.

Finds your WiiM

Set up Wi-Fi from your phone with a QR code. The Tab5 finds the WiiM on its own.

Private

No account and no password on the device. The WiiM's certificate is pinned.

Also on a computer

The same app runs on Windows (and, experimental, Raspberry Pi, Linux, macOS).

Install it from this page

One USB flash. After that, the Tab5 updates itself over Wi-Fi.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Connect the Tab5 with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Push the power button one time to turn on the Tab5. Then push and hold it until the green LED flashes (download mode).
  3. Click Install and select the port of the Tab5. When the browser asks, leave Erase device off: your settings and favorites stay.
  4. Push the power button. Scan the QR code on the screen with your phone to set up Wi-Fi and your WiiM.

For the M5Stack Tab5. One file, written at address 0x0. The browser does everything.

This browser cannot talk to USB devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. The install needs a secure page (https).

Prefer a tool? Download wiim-remote-tab5.bin and write it at 0x0 with ESPWebTool or esptool --chip esp32p4 write-flash 0x0 wiim-remote-tab5.bin.

Windows: wiimscreen-windows-x64.zip on the release page.

How it works

Only the local interfaces of the WiiM. Some are not documented by WiiM, so a WiiM firmware update can change them.

HTTP API

Status, track, volume, presets and commands, over HTTPS. The Tab5 pins the WiiM's certificate on first contact.

UPnP play queues

The service the WiiM Home app uses. It plays albums, playlists, favorites and search results.

Nothing in the cloud

Only album art, searches and the update check leave your network. The WiiM plays Qobuz and Deezer with its own login.
